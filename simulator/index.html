How a score is computed

  1. Prompt. The model gets a driving scenario and a fixed system prompt that lists the propositions and actions.
  2. Parse (GLM2FSA). The response becomes an ordered list of guarded rules: a one-state automaton with prioritized transitions. A phrase that names no known proposition becomes a free Boolean atom.
  3. Model check. The controller is composed with an unconstrained environment (fair: the light is eventually green and clear) and checked with nuXmv against the 15 LTL specifications. If no rule applies, any action is possible.
  4. Score. The score is the number of satisfied specifications. During training, higher-scored responses are preferred in DPO pairs.
SpecFormulaMeaning

The simulator evaluates free atoms as false and checks the state-action invariants (φ2, φ3, φ5, φ6, φ9, φ11, φ12, φ14, φ15) at run time. The 15 verdicts next to each lane come from the model checker, not from the simulation.
